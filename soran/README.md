## Instagram'da yazdigim aciklama:

#yosakoi is a unique Japanese dance, first started in Kochi in 1954. It's very energetic and dancers generally use #naruko (bird rattle), and wear a yukata and happi. While there are many types of Yosakoi from different regions in Japan, my favorite is #yosakoisoran

Yosakoi Soran is an annual festival held in Sapporo, Hokkaido, first started in 1992. Besides from the Sapporo Snow Festival, it's one of the few main events bringing tourists to the city; with around 2 million spectators and over 25000 participants. 

Anyone can attend this festival from any age, both local and foreigners (there were even participants from Estonia!). There are only 2 requirements for participating teams: to play the Naruko instrument, and the music being played should include a part of "Soran Bushi". a fishermen's sea shanty also originated from Hokkaido. This is no suprising, since Hokkaido is one of the most seafearing cities in Japan. 

Its main events are held on a stage set up in Odori park infront of the Sapporo TV Tower. There are also teams parading on the roads both sides of the park. Also throughout the city, in the parks and schoolyards there are teams showing their dances. The festival has a competition for those who wish to participate. 

I was extremely lucky to be in Sapporo that weekend for the #yosakoisoranfestival
